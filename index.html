Understanding the System

The Four Years of High School

Each year of high school serves a different purpose. Knowing what to expect helps you plan smarter and stay on track.

9th Grade

Freshman Year

Your transition year. Adjust to the environment, learn how classes work, and begin building your academic record.

10th Grade

Sophomore Year

Continue core classes and begin exploring interests through extracurricular activities and electives.

11th Grade

Junior Year

The most academically important year. Take your most challenging classes, build strong grades, and begin college prep.

12th Grade

Senior Year

Complete graduation requirements, apply to college, and prepare for life after high school.

How You Graduate & Succeed

Credits & GPA Explained

GPA reflects discipline and consistency — not just intelligence. Colleges look at your entire four-year trend.

Choosing the Right Level

Types of Classes

Classes come in different difficulty levels. Choosing wisely balances challenge with wellbeing.

Standard

Regular

Standard level classes that meet all graduation requirements. A great foundation.

GPA: standard scale
College Prep

CP

Designed to prepare students for college-level work and expectations.

GPA: standard scale
Advanced

Honors

Faster-paced, more in-depth content. Requires strong study habits and commitment.

GPA: +0.5 boost
College-Level

AP

College-level courses with an end-of-year exam. Passing may earn real college credit.

GPA: +1.0 boost

💡 Taking challenging classes matters — but balance is key. Don't overload yourself.

Your Journey

High School → College Timeline

A roadmap of what to focus on each year so you're always moving forward.

Freshman Year · 9th Grade

Settle In & Explore

  • Adjust to high school life and expectations
  • Learn how GPA and credits work
  • Explore clubs and activities
Sophomore Year · 10th Grade

Build Involvement

  • Commit to 1–2 extracurricular activities
  • Improve and maintain your GPA
  • Begin thinking about future goals
Junior Year · 11th Grade

Academic Peak

  • Take your most challenging classes (AP/Honors)
  • Start researching colleges
  • Build relationships with teachers for recommendations
Senior Year · 12th Grade

Apply & Launch

  • Complete all graduation requirements
  • Submit college applications (Common App, etc.)
  • Write personal essays and gather recommendations
